QUESTIONS YOU MIGHT HAVE

have it all figured out.

I don’t know where AI would help. Can we still talk?+

Yes. Start with a task that takes too long or causes mistakes. We look at how it works today and discuss whether AI, a simpler software change or another approach makes sense.

Will we have to replace our existing software?+

We first look at working with the tools you already use. What can be connected depends on the access and features those tools provide. We check that before agreeing a build.

Can our business information stay private?+

We discuss what information is involved, who should see it and where it may be processed. A setup on your own systems may be an option. Privacy depends on the complete setup and needs to be checked.

What will it cost?+

It depends on the task, your existing systems and the ongoing running costs. We agree the scope and explain the costs before starting. A small first test can help you decide whether a larger build is worthwhile.

Do you sell a ready-made product or build something for us?+

Our main service is building and connecting tools around your needs. Our project examples show different approaches. Some are demonstrations or pilots, so we confirm what is available and what your business would need.
