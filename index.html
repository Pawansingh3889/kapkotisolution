A small note on your privacy

The AI assistant processes your messages using OpenRouter to help clarify your problem. If you choose “Send to Pawan”, Kapkoti Solution stores your conversation, the brief you approve, your name and your chosen contact details in a private Cloudflare database and sends a copy through Resend to pawan@kapkotisolution.com for reviewing and following up on your request. Contact details entered in the form are not sent to the AI.

We use these details to respond to your request, not to sign you up for marketing. Please don't share passwords, financial account details or other sensitive information. To request deletion, email pawan@kapkotisolution.com with your submission reference. Unsubmitted conversations are held only in this page's memory and are cleared when you reload it. Short-lived abuse-prevention records use a hashed network address.
